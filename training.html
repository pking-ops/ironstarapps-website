Training

Training that builds confidence, awareness, and readiness.

From on-site instruction to firearms training and team development, we help equip churches with practical skills and a calm, capable response mindset.

Training Categories

🏫

On-Site Training

Training delivered in your own environment so team members learn in the place where they actually serve.

🎯

Firearms Training

Professional firearms instruction for qualified team members with a focus on safety, proficiency, decision-making, and readiness.

🧭

Scenario-Based Drills

Walkthroughs and practical exercises to help teams think, move, communicate, and respond together.

📞

Communication & Response

Training that helps teams improve observation, communication, emergency action flow, and response coordination.

Built around your ministry

Not one-size-fits-all training.

Every church is different. Size, layout, children’s programs, service times, and volunteer structure all matter. We tailor the training to your people and your setting so it makes sense to the team who will actually use it.

Examples of training topics

  • Situational awareness in the church environment
  • Greeter and usher awareness concepts
  • Children’s area protection considerations
  • Medical response and first actions
  • Response roles for designated team members
  • Working alongside arriving law enforcement
